Code of Conduct

A sample set of expectations for making the summit welcoming, respectful, and safe for everyone.

Sample event policy. This draft is for layout review. Confirm the reporting contacts, response process, and enforcement approach with the event organizers before publishing.

Sample draft · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Our commitment

We want the NexGen Agentic AI Summit to be a welcoming place for learning, discussion, and professional connection. Everyone attending, speaking, sponsoring, exhibiting, or working at the event is expected to help maintain a respectful environment.

Expected behavior

  • Treat people with respect and consideration.
  • Listen to different perspectives and disagree with ideas respectfully.
  • Follow instructions from venue staff and event organizers.
  • Ask before photographing, recording, or sharing another person’s identifiable information.

Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, threats, persistent disruption, and unwelcome recording are not acceptable at the event or in its official online spaces.

How to report a concern

If you experience or witness behavior that concerns you, contact an event staff member or the designated event safety contact. Before publishing, add a monitored phone number or email address, explain where staff can be found during the event, and describe how reports will be handled.

Response

Event organizers should review reports promptly, prioritize immediate safety, and take appropriate action. Possible responses may include a warning, removal from an activity or venue, or other steps consistent with event policy and applicable requirements. Document the actual response process before publishing this policy.

Scope

This sample policy is intended to cover summit sessions, receptions, official networking activities, and event communication channels. Define the final policy’s scope and who is responsible for applying it.
