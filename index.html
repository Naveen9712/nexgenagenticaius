About The Event

The NexGen Agentic AI Summit, New York Edition, is a high-impact, in-person event focused on the practical realities of deploying agentic AI inside the enterprise: architecture, orchestration, governance, and return on investment.

  • Architecture
  • Orchestration
  • Governance
  • Return on investment

In 2027, agentic AI has moved past the proof-of-concept stage in nearly every industry, yet most organizations are still struggling to translate pilots into production systems that hold up under real operational load.

Fewer than a third of enterprises running agentic pilots have a clear framework for measuring ROI, and even fewer have solved for the orchestration and governance challenges that come with agents making decisions at scale.

This summit provides a strategic and technical deep dive into building agentic AI systems that work in production, from multi-agent orchestration and tool integration to cost management and enterprise governance. Attendees will gain insights into how leading organizations are moving beyond the pilot phase to build agentic systems that deliver measurable business outcomes.

Join enterprise AI leaders, engineering heads, and operations executives as they define what production-grade agentic AI actually looks like heading into 2027.

Join the NexGen Agentic AI Summit, New York Edition, and be part of the conversations defining how enterprises deploy, scale, and govern autonomous AI systems that deliver real results.

This exclusive, in-person gathering brings together enterprise AI leaders, engineering heads, and operations executives to explore what it actually takes to move agentic AI from pilot to production.
